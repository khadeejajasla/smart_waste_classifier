About this project

Model: MobileNetV2 with transfer learning (pretrained on ImageNet, fine-tuned for waste).

Dataset: RealWaste, about 4,750 real landfill photos across 9 material types.

Validation accuracy: around 85%.

Note: Plastic, Metal and Glass can look similar in photos, so these are the most common mix-ups.
